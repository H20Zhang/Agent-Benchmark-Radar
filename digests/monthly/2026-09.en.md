## Monthly judgment: separate failure stages hidden by aggregate scores

The period clarifies three boundaries: correct updating does not establish appropriate memory use; document hits do not establish complete evidence discovery or faithful writing; executable code does not establish correct formulation, variables or business rules. More granular measurement does not demonstrate a universally superior architecture. Historical backfills also prevent interpreting acceptance month as a sudden research turn.

## Memory: score updating, preservation, use and action separately

[MemProbe](../../benchmarks/memprobe-stability-plasticity.en.md) separates valid updating, resistance to unreliable information and source fidelity; [LoCoMo-Conv](../../benchmarks/locomo-conv.en.md) fixes histories and evidence while changing requests; [MemCalib](../../benchmarks/memcalib.en.md) supplies memory and tests excessive or insufficient influence; [DolphinBench](../../benchmarks/dolphinbench.en.md) examines simulated actions under frozen memory. These are not interchangeable pieces of one memory ranking.

MemProbe's main experiment shares a Gemini-3-Flash-preview final reader across 56 episodes and 224 nominal probes. A-MEM scores 91.1% on updating versus Cognee's 75.0%, but 85.7% on preservation versus 88.4%. Unequal retrieval budgets, embeddings, metadata access and ingestion success make these whole-configuration observations. [Source: v1, Tables 1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30558v1)

Next, inspect complete stores, retrieved evidence, answers with supplied-correct-evidence and final actions separately on the same tasks, matching ingestion success and evidence budgets. MemCalib's executable scorer remained unverified in its recorded review; DolphinBench's task certification depends on the selected agent, not a model-independent ceiling.

## Retrieval: candidate recall, evidence chains and faithful writing need separate contracts

[Q2D-Web](../../benchmarks/q2d-web.en.md) evaluates first-stage retrieval for agent queries; [CrossModalQA](../../benchmarks/crossmodalqa.en.md) tests multimodal evidence composition; [Mr.LHDR](../../benchmarks/mr-lhdr.en.md) checks dependency-consistent intermediate statements. [LiveDRBench](../../benchmarks/livedrbench.en.md) restores an earlier structured-discovery instrument; [ClaimProbe](../../benchmarks/claimprobe.en.md) fixes retrieved evidence to audit writer-side claim–source relations.

With EDR/GPT-5.5 writing, GPT-5.4-mini auditing, 100 tasks and micro-averaged default metrics accepting partial support, the unsupported-claim rate falls from 15.89% to 5.02%. The separate RACE evaluation falls from 0.544 to 0.536 overall and 0.510 to 0.470 in readability. RACE's unresolved 50/100 task-count conflict and unmatched writing budgets prevent pooling these evaluations. [Source: v1, Tables 1–2 and 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.28643v1)

Next, fix queries, corpus snapshots, candidate pools and writer-visible evidence in successive interventions. Q2D-Web's private inputs limit independent auditing; ClaimProbe's support shortlists can miss evidence; Mr.LHDR checks submitted statements rather than hidden reasoning or actual retrieval.

## Data agents: formulation and rule translation precede executable answers

[StatFormBench](../../benchmarks/statformbench.en.md) checks statistical categories, variables and roles first; [DI-Bench](../../benchmarks/di-bench-data-intelligence.en.md) requires retrieving rules and implementing them; [HybridDeepResearch](../../benchmarks/hybrid-deep-research.en.md) examines transfer of entities, constraints and candidate sets between SQL and search. They expose what to compute, which rules apply and how sources connect.

Across DI-Bench's 175 rule tasks, four-model mean accuracy changes from 32.5% with retrieved rules to 32.7% with supplied rules, versus 52.9% without rule adjustments. The last condition changes target metrics and reference answers; it is not a retrieval ablation against an unchanged answer. [Source: v1, Table 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05776v1)

Next, supply a common executor with expert-confirmed formulations, correct rules and correct intermediates in separate conditions. DI-Bench covers synthetic rules in two domains and its rule-necessity description conflicts with 82% pairing validity; StatFormBench's exact variable-string matching can penalize equivalent expressions; HybridDeepResearch's retries and unequal realized costs limit orchestration attribution.
