# AgentDS: data-agent evaluation should compare human-only, AI-only, and human-AI collaboration

[中文](agentds.md) | **English** · [Home](../README.en.md) · [Benchmark Library](../library/README.en.md)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19005)

## What it measures

AgentDS uses 17 data-science challenges across six industries with 29 teams and 80 participants, comparing AI-only and human-AI collaboration modes on synthetic enterprise-pattern data with hidden leaderboard evaluation.

## Compared with what

Most benchmarks rank agents only. AgentDS makes augmentation itself an evaluation object: how much can AI complete alone, when does collaboration help, and where is human judgment still load-bearing?

## Score boundary

Challenge scores support performance under the current participant pool, hidden tests, and collaboration protocol. Small team samples, synthetic data, and human-skill variance prevent causal conclusions such as “agents replace data scientists.”

## Fair comparison conditions

Align challenge release, participant selection, AI tools/models, time budget, collaboration rules, and hidden evaluator. AI-only, human-AI, and human-only require separate tracks.

## Next evaluation coordinate

The next step measures longitudinal team productivity, review burden, error severity, and decision impact, asking whether AI reduces work or merely shifts it.
