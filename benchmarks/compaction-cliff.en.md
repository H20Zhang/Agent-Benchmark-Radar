# The Compaction Cliff

- **Measurement object:** Whether safety constraints survive repeated compaction, decomposition, and retrieval under a bounded context and still govern later behavior.
- **Closest predecessor:** MaRS types memory but uses one utility; LLMLingua-2 and production compactors are type-blind. This work extends exact constraint preservation across all three context-management operators.
- **Decisive evidence:** Sonnet `/compact` falls from 0.53 constraint recall after one round to 0.10 after five; TypeCompact stabilizes at 0.96, TypeDecompose has 0% locality violations, and TypeRetrieve reaches 100% recall@50.
- **Score ceiling:** The evidence supports typed retention under these protocols, not a universal guarantee that long-running agents become safe.
- **Strongest confounder:** Behavioral comparisons are not token-matched; TypeCompact retains more context, and every guarantee inherits classifier false negatives.
- **Remaining gap:** Online typing, enterprise distributions, inter-agent memory, and token-matched behavior.
- **Genealogy:** With MPBench, InjecMEM, and Utility Under Attack, it locates memory safety at distinct write, retrieval, and compaction stages; `map_delta=reinforces`.
