# RAG Collapse

- **Measurement object:** Whether independent sources are progressively displaced when a fixed model repeatedly retrieves model-authored sources in recursive loops.
- **Closest predecessor:** Model-collapse work studies recursive training; this keeps weights fixed and moves the feedback loop into retrieval context.
- **Decisive evidence:** 79.6% of 1,528 simulations collapse overall, with high collapse rates under Replace-All, Replace-One, and Search protocols.
- **Score ceiling:** The result establishes collapse in synthetic retrieval loops, not that the live web already exhibits the same behavior.
- **Strongest confounder:** The same model family writes and later reads sources, many seeds are single-run, and collapse/quality are model-judged.
- **Remaining gap:** Longitudinal live-web evidence, cross-model authorship, style/content separation, and human labels.
- **Genealogy:** It makes corpus provenance and feedback dynamics a RAG validity coordinate; `map_delta=reinforces`.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22118
